1. ASSIGNMENT OBJECTIVE & BRIEF
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This project develops a professional digital marketing campaign for social change to raise awareness, encourage behavioral change, and inspire community engagement addressing Bangladesh's critical social problem: Urban Hyper-Centralization & Regional Brain Drain ("Why Dhaka Keeps Growing").

The campaign demonstrates core marketing fundamentals, branding, communication strategy, STP, 4Ps, SWOT analysis, AIDA model, omnichannel digital planning, and creative content design.
